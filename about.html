Who We Are

We are Tea, Juliette, and Lilly, a team of three students working together to create DrainSight, an AI-powered storm-drain awareness tool.

We believe that technology can be used to address real-world challenges, even when the people creating it are still students.

Through DrainSight, we are combining creativity, problem-solving, and artificial intelligence to explore a new approach to stormwater awareness.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make it easier for people to recognize visible debris around storm drains and understand why stormwater management matters.

We want to demonstrate how artificial intelligence can support environmental awareness and inspire people to pay closer attention to their communities.

Our belief: You don't have to be an expert to start working toward meaningful change.

How DrainSight Started

We wanted to explore how artificial intelligence could be used to address a practical environmental challenge.

Storm drains are easy to overlook, but they play an important role in managing rainwater. We decided to focus on identifying visible debris around drains using photographs.

We began developing an AI image classifier and built a website where users could upload photographs and explore the model's predictions.

Our Goals for the Future

DrainSight is still growing, and we hope to continue improving it.

Our goals include collecting more training images, improving prediction accuracy, testing the tool in different conditions, and sharing what we learn.

We hope DrainSight encourages other students to use their ideas and skills to explore challenges in their own communities.
