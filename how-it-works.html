How DrainSight Works

Learn how our AI-powered storm drain scanner helps identify visible drainage concerns through simple photographs.

What Is DrainSight?

DrainSight is a student-created website designed to help people recognize potential storm drain problems in Westport, Connecticut.

Our website uses artificial intelligence to examine photographs of storm drains and identify whether they appear clear, contain visible debris, or cannot be confidently classified.

STEP 01

Take a Photograph

Find a storm drain that you can safely photograph from a sidewalk or another safe public area. Make sure the drain is clearly visible. Never enter the road or touch a drain to get a photo.

STEP 02

Upload Your Photo

Visit the DrainSight homepage and upload your storm drain photograph using the photo upload feature.

You can also enter a general location to help describe where the drain is. Avoid including private addresses or personal information.

STEP 03

Let the AI Analyze It

Click the Analyze Photograph button. Our image recognition model, trained using Google Teachable Machine, examines the image and compares it to patterns learned during training.

The AI then displays its predicted classification and confidence scores.

STEP 04

Understand Your Results

Clear

The drain appears relatively free of visible debris. This does not guarantee that water can flow properly underneath the surface.

Visible Debris

The AI detects visual patterns associated with leaves, trash, or other debris near a storm drain. This could indicate a concern worth reviewing.

Uncertain

The image may be difficult to classify, or the model may recognize patterns associated with its uncertain training examples. Consider taking another clear photograph from a safe location.

How Accurate Is DrainSight?

DrainSight is an early-stage student AI prototype. Its predictions are not always correct. Lighting, camera angles, image quality, and limited training examples can affect results.

A high confidence score does not guarantee that the prediction is accurate. DrainSight is an educational observation tool, not an official drainage inspection service.
