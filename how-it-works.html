How DrainSight Works

DrainSight uses artificial intelligence to help people recognize visible storm drain conditions from photographs.

01 — Take a Photograph

Take a clear photograph of a storm drain from a safe location, such as a sidewalk. Never enter a roadway or approach moving traffic to take a picture.

02 — Upload Your Image

Visit the DrainSight homepage and select your photograph. The website displays a preview so you can make sure you selected the correct image.

03 — Let AI Analyze It

DrainSight uses an image classification model trained to recognize three categories: Clear, Visible Debris, and Uncertain.

04 — Understand Your Results

Clear means the drain appears unobstructed. Visible Debris means the photograph shows potential obstructions such as leaves or litter. Uncertain means the image cannot be reliably categorized.

05 — Take the Next Step

If you notice a possible drainage concern, consider reporting it through an appropriate local government channel. Do not attempt to remove heavy debris or enter storm drains.

Important Reminder

DrainSight is a student-built prototype. Its AI results are estimates and may be incorrect. It cannot inspect underground pipes, confirm drainage performance, or predict whether flooding will occur.
